Company Overview

KLJ Resources imports, markets, and distributes petrochemicals, derivatives, and solvents in India, and manufactures specialty chemicals and de-aromatized solvents. Its products include paraffins, alcohols, aromatics, ketones, monomers, and acetates, and it blends base oils. The company has sales above 280,000 tonnes per year and a pan-India marketing network with storage, drumming, and distribution facilities.

Company Brief
